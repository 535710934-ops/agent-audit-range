Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-15 · Greybox (individual developer, operated by Wang Longjun, China)

This site and the Greybox Self-Check Toolkit are designed to require as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what is handled, by whom, and how.

1. What we collect (and mostly, what we don't)

  • This website collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no forms. It is a static page.
  • The toolkit collects nothing. The scanner runs locally on your machine, reads only the network responses of the MCP server you point it at, and sends nothing anywhere. There is no telemetry, no license check, no phone-home.
  • Purchase data is handled by Creem (our Merchant of Record). When you buy the toolkit, Creem processes your payment and the legally required tax/KYC data under its own privacy policy: creem.io/privacy. We never see your card details. We receive only what is needed to deliver the product and support you: your email address and basic order information.

2. Audit-request information

If you email us for a free snapshot audit or any paid audit work, we use the information you send (target hostnames, authorization notes, your name/email) solely to respond, verify authorization, and deliver the result. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties. You may ask us to delete it at any time by email.

3. What we never do

  • We never execute your tools, read your business data, or change your configuration during a passive check.
  • We never publish anything identifying you or your infrastructure without your written permission.

4. Retention & your rights

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your request. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information you have sent us: email 535710934@qq.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Material changes will not retroactively reduce your protections.
